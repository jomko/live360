About LIVE 360

We believe culture comes alive when people, ideas and inspiration meet. Founded in Lviv in 2020, LIVE 360 is a Ukrainian non-governmental organisation created to open up new possibilities for music, art, creative communities and cultural exchange. We believe in bold ideas, openness and collaboration across borders.

Let's collaborate

The most exciting cultural stories are created together. We welcome collaboration with festivals, cultural organisations, artists and international partners. By bringing together ideas, experience and resources, we hope to spark new events, creative encounters and projects that connect people.
